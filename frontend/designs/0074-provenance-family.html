Provenance family filter design · 0074 · for review

Two new values in the findings table's existing provenance filter, and one read-only row in the expanded finding. Nothing here changes a finding's validation status, confidence or any voter input: the tier a finding came from is orthogonal to how much it is trusted.

Every specimen below is drawn with tokens.css + components.css and is the pixel reference for the implementation (the 0074 Playwright spec screenshots the elements marked data-testid here and in the app, in the same browser, and compares them).

The group is the shared ChipGroup: a legend, then shared Chips. "All" first, then the two family values, then the exact tiers present in the audit, sorted. The exact tiers keep their exact meaning.
